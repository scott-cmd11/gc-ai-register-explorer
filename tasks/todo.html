Assumptions

  • Primary users are journalists, researchers, public-sector staff, policy analysts, and civic-tech users trying to understand reported federal AI systems.
  • The main user action is to browse/search/filter the public register and inspect details, with CSV export as a secondary action.
  • The product should feel like a credible civic data explorer, not a marketing landing page or experimental demo.
